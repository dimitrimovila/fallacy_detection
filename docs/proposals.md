* Remove the CUDA 13 libraries from the environment `vllm-0.30.0` on the cluster. They are left over from a wrong installation and nothing needs them, but any kernel compiled on the fly would again use the `nvcc` 13 among them, which is what made job 1686437 fail (today avoided with `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0`). Not decided yet.
* Reduce what is saved of the logprobs. The pilot left 13 GB of `raw.jsonl` and 15 GB of `cache.sqlite` for its 6960 calls, and the full run of phase 2 covers the 773 items of `items.csv`, not 50. Three causes add up. Every generated token carries its 20 alternatives (`top_logprobs` is 20 for every call), the reasoning tokens of the `_think` entries included, up to 8192 per call. Every token and every alternative is saved with `token`, `logprob` and `bytes` (the UTF-8 bytes of the token as a list of integers, or `null`). And the whole block is saved twice, in `raw_response.logprobs` and inside `raw_response.raw`, the payload of the server kept whole, both in `raw.jsonl` and in the cache, which also writes its JSON with a space after every separator. Of this block the parser reads only the `token` of every entry, to find the last `"answer": "` (or `"scheme": "`), and the alternatives at that one position. The options, from the one that loses nothing to the one that loses most: (a) leave `choices[].logprobs` out of `raw`, where it is an exact copy of `logprobs`; (b) leave `bytes` out, since it repeats the text of `token`, except for a token that splits a multibyte character, which the parser does not need; (c) compress, gzip for `raw.jsonl` and zlib for the `response` column of the cache: nothing is lost, but the files can no longer be read as text, and the resume, which today skips a line cut off by a crash, has to tolerate a gzip member cut off in the same way; (d) keep the alternatives only at the answer token: the client would have to find the final object, that is, parse, against the separation between inference and parsing, and a later parser could not read another position, the `confidence` for example; (e) lower `top_logprobs`: it is a generation parameter, so it changes the cache key, and the fewer the alternatives, the more rows are `logprob_partial` (stage one has nine admitted answers). (a) and (b) leave what the parser reads unchanged and do not make the client interpret anything; (c) can be added if they are not enough. Before choosing, measure on a few lines of the pilot how much each cause weighs, per entry. Whatever is chosen applies to new runs: the pilot files stay as they are and the parser keeps reading them, the entries already in the cache stay readable, and section 2.5 of spec 04 changes with it. Not decided yet.
