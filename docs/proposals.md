* For the full parser (spec 05): 5 of the 298 hard answers of `qwen3_8_27b` at sample 0, at temperature 0, are not the answer with the highest `p_logprob`, and none on the other three entries of the pilot (section 9 of the pilot report). In all five the answer given and the highest mass are less than 0.001 apart (for example `yes` 0.3534 and `no` 0.3533, answer `no`). To be checked on `raw.jsonl`, at the answer position of those five calls. Two causes are possible. In `logprob_masses` a token that is a prefix of two admitted answers is credited only to the first one it matches, in the order of the options: `n` goes to `no` and never to `na` (to `negative` for ad hominem CQ1). And a mass sums every token that begins an answer, while the choice at temperature 0 takes the single most probable token, so near a tie the two can differ with no error in the parser. If the token chosen has the highest logprob of its position, it is the second; if the difference comes from a shared prefix, the rule of the first match has to change (split the token between the answers it begins, or leave it out and mark the row `logprob_partial`). Not decided yet.
* Reduce further what is saved of the logprobs, if the stored form of spec 04, section 2.5, is not enough. * How to treat `cannot_be_determined` at stage two, an open problem after the pilot (29 September 2026). The stopping rule is triggered: 15 of the 48 questions have more than 50 per cent `cannot_be_determined` on at least one entry, or more than 50 per cent `na` (cause to effect CQ2.1), 38 (question, entry) pairs; share of `cannot_be_determined` at stage two, Qwen 6.0 per cent with reasoning off and 19.8 with reasoning on, Gemma 23.4 and 27.2 (section 1 of the pilot report). How the models read it: at sample 0, 223 answers are `cannot_be_determined`; 200 justifications say that the text gives "no information" on the point or "does not mention", "state" or "specify" it, the other 23 say the same in other words; one treats inference as a criterion ("does not explicitly state or imply"); none refers to outside knowledge. The models read the answer as "the text does not say it explicitly", also where the text allows the inference: on analogy CQ1 the differences between the two cases the text names are left undetermined because the text "does not mention" them. The cause is in the prompt: `stage2_v1` defines `cannot_be_determined` as "no information at all on the point" and asks for an answer from the text alone, without saying whether what can be inferred from the text counts as information. Three levels are to be told apart: stated in the text, inferable from the text, requiring world knowledge or judgement; the models used `cannot_be_determined` for the second and the third. Related: where Gemma with reasoning off answers `cannot_be_determined`, Qwen with reasoning off answers `no` in 68 per cent of the 71 cases, and `no` is not neutral, since its direction changes from question to question; the per-CQ annotation of the pilot items has one `idk` in 61 answers. Options: (a) read the answer in the aggregation by burden of proof (Gordon, Prakken and Walton 2007, section 5; Walton, Reed and Macagno 2008, pp. 32 to 34): on an exception, a question whose objection must be shown by whoever raises it, the traversal takes the arc on which the exception does not hold; on an assumption, a question on something the argument needs, the system abstains, or recovers a verdict from the probabilities, from the other models or with the branch policy; this needs a one-time classification of the 48 questions, with the reason and, for every exception, the arc on which it does not hold; the prompt does not change and the pilot data stay valid; (b) a stage-two prompt that says that information inferable from the text counts as information; (c) a stage-two prompt that admits the evaluator's judgement and general knowledge, as the annotators do. (b) and (c) change the prompt, and so what is measured, and need a new pilot. Not as the main rule: reading `cannot_be_determined` on an assumption as the arc of the fallacy, which turns missing information into an accusation; at most a variant for comparison. Checks still open: the text of the reasoning in `raw.jsonl` on the cluster, to see whether the entries with reasoning considered an inference before answering `cannot_be_determined`; the rates of gpt oss and K2 Horizon. On 4 October 2026 the prompts v2 took options (b) and (c); whether (a) is still needed depends on the second pilot.
