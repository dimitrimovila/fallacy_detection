* Proposals to reformulate the critical questions: they are in `docs/cq_proposals.md`, seven proposals on eight questions and six schemes, to be applied only after the v0 baseline.
* Version the gold instead of overwriting it, the current v1.0 and v2.0 after Dumitru's annotation, because the numbers of the diagnosis (0.374 pipeline, 0.565 zero-shot, 0.834 scheme) are anchored to v1.0. Not decided yet.
* Freeze the gold scheme before the runs of phase 2 and collect the scheme corrections found during annotation in a separate list instead of editing them inside items.csv, because the scheme decides which questions are asked while the verdict does not. Not decided yet.
* Let the pilot job skip, when it is resubmitted with the same run id, the entries already complete: those whose planned calls all have an answered line in `raw.jsonl`, as `calls_per_model` of the manifest counts them. Today the job relaunches the server and the smoke test of every entry, and `execute` finds nothing to do for the complete ones: a few minutes of loading per entry on a four-hour job. Worth doing if resubmissions become frequent. Not decided yet.
* Remove the CUDA 13 libraries from the environment `vllm-0.30.0` on the cluster. They are left over from a wrong installation and nothing needs them, but any kernel compiled on the fly would again use the `nvcc` 13 among them, which is what made job 1686437 fail (today avoided with `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0`). Packages present on 27 September 2026: `nvidia-cublas 13.1.1.3`, `nvidia-cuda-cccl 13.3.4.3.1`, `nvidia-cuda-crt 13.4.92`, `nvidia-cuda-cupti 13.0.85`, `nvidia-cuda-nvcc 13.4.92`, `nvidia-cuda-nvdisasm 13.4.92`, `nvidia-cuda-nvrtc 13.0.88`, `nvidia-cuda-runtime 13.0.96`, `nvidia-cudnn-cu13`, `nvidia-cufft 12.0.0.61`, `nvidia-cufile 1.15.1.6`, `nvidia-curand 10.4.0.35`, `nvidia-cusolver 12.0.4.66`, `nvidia-cusparse 12.6.3.3`, `nvidia-cusparselt-cu13`, `nvidia-nccl-cu13`, `nvidia-nvjitlink 13.4.92`, `nvidia-nvshmem-cu13`, `nvidia-nvtx 13.0.85`, `nvidia-nvvm 13.4.92`. `nvidia-cutlass-dsl-libs-cu13` stays: it depends on `cuda-python`, and the effect of removing it has not been checked. The folder `~/.cache/flashinfer/0.6.18.post1` holds modules compiled with CUDA 13 and is emptied at the same time. To be done after the pilot, ending with `pip check` and a new smoke test. Not decided yet.
* Reduce further what is saved of the logprobs, if the stored form of spec 04, section 2.5, is not enough. The pilot left 13 GB of `raw.jsonl` and 15 GB of `cache.sqlite` for its 6960 calls, and the full run of phase 2 covers the 773 items of `items.csv`, not 50. The copy of the logprobs inside `raw` and the `bytes` fields are no longer saved, about 70 per cent less per line by the measure on the pilot. What is left is the block itself: every generated token carries its 20 alternatives (`top_logprobs` is 20 for every call), the reasoning tokens included, up to 8192 per call, while the parser reads only the `token` of every entry, to find the last `"answer": "` (or `"scheme": "`), and the alternatives at that one position. The cache also writes its JSON with a space after every separator. The options left, from the one that loses nothing to the one that loses most: (c) compress, gzip for `raw.jsonl` and zlib for the `response` column of the cache: nothing is lost, but the files can no longer be read as text, and the resume, which today skips a line cut off by a crash, has to tolerate a gzip member cut off in the same way; (d) keep the alternatives only at the answer token: the client would have to find the final object, that is, parse, against the separation between inference and parsing, and a later parser could not read another position, the `confidence` for example; (e) lower `top_logprobs`: it is a generation parameter, so it changes the cache key, and the fewer the alternatives, the more rows are `logprob_partial` (stage one has nine admitted answers). Of the three, only (c) loses nothing. Whatever is chosen applies to new runs: the files written before stay as they are and must stay readable, and section 2.5 of spec 04 changes with it. Not decided yet.
